How to use

  • Press Start Keyboard, then scan the QR code with your phone (or open the URL and enter the 6-digit code).
  • On the phone page, tap the text box and type — or tap your keyboard's mic (Gboard) to dictate in English or Khmer.
  • Press Send to PC to type the text into whatever Windows app is focused. Direct keys (Enter, Backspace, arrows) apply immediately.
  • Wi-Fi, USB tethering, and wired LAN all work. Keep the phone and PC on the same network; nothing leaves your LAN.
